\begin{abstract}
Aggregated large language model services expose declared model identifiers while abstracting the backend configurations that fulfill client requests. We present HuePrint, a lightweight framework that audits brokered endpoint consistency via short structured color elicitation and continuous geometric distribution comparison without model weights or token probabilities. Across 34,528 cloud responses spanning 56 endpoints and 12 backends, HuePrint lowers 30-call-per-side equal error rates from 4.52--5.23 to 0.94--1.00 percent on the development cohort and from 6.25--8.04 to 3.49--5.00 percent across 20 locked endpoints, reaching zero at moderate depth. Continuous and multi-scale RGB distances halve shallow-census error vs. exact-hex divergence (5.36 vs. 10.71 percent), cut cross-provider error from 16.67 to 5.93 percent, retain zero ranking error across three-week and cross-gateway checks where equality tests reject up to 42.86 percent of genuine pairs, and bound numerical perturbations. Unified Lipschitz bounds and estimator diagnostics guide calibration: self-centering lowers clean held-out false rejections to 10 percent, and U-statistic energy ratios eliminate the 256-draw bootstrap. Within-family, mixed-routing, and canary audits below four cents median query cost inform cloud assurance guidelines.
\end{abstract}
