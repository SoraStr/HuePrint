\begin{abstract}
Aggregated large language model (LLM) services expose declared identifiers while hiding backend configurations. We present HuePrint, a black-box framework that audits brokered endpoint consistency via structured color elicitation and geometric comparison without weights or token probabilities. Across 38,688 cloud responses (56 endpoints, 12 backends), HuePrint cuts 30-call/side equal error rates (EERs) over word baselines from 4.52--5.23 to 0.94--1.00 percent on development models and from 6.25--8.04 to 3.49--5.00 percent across 20 locked endpoints, reaching 0.00 percent at 60--150 calls/side. On those 20 endpoints, HuePrint averages 5.18 percent within-family EER across all ten balanced 30-call splits (0.00--7.89 percent), outperforming open-set LLMmap (10.53--34.39 percent, 1--3 traces) and cost-matched long-form kernels (11.09--19.47 percent); relative to LLMmap, it also avoids safety-triggering probes and runs over 15,000 times faster locally. Regularized RGB energy lowers shallow EER (5.36 vs. 10.71 percent exact-hex and 6.42 percent string MMD), while hybrid scoring cuts cross-provider EER from 16.67 to 5.93 percent. In offline replay, two-stage confirmation cuts false alerts from 14 to 1 across 100 same-label windows and flags 186/190 within-family substitutions under one cent expected cost.
\end{abstract}
